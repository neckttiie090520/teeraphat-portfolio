## Design Context

### Users

Recruiters and hiring teams evaluating AI/software engineering candidates, plus technical collaborators and prospective clients. Their main task is to assess Teeraphat's role, project ownership, and practical delivery evidence, then contact him or open his CV.

### Brand Personality

Precise, practical, inventive. The tone is confident and straightforward without inflated language. English copy should remain simple and honest about working-level communication.

### Aesthetic Direction

An asymmetric Swiss International grid with oversized typographic hierarchy, visible rules, generous white space, and restrained screen-print texture. Use an off-white/white field, near-black ink, and Swiss Red (#FF3000) for functional emphasis. Keep geometry flat and square, with one consistent sans-serif family. References guide the information hierarchy and motion craft; do not copy their layouts or distinctive assets. Avoid generic portfolio cards, glass effects, dark gradients, decorative 3D, and unverified project imagery.

### Design Principles

1. Put evidence and shipped work ahead of self-description.
2. Make end-to-end ownership legible from problem to release.
3. Let motion clarify sequence and focus; keep a fully usable reduced-motion path.
4. Keep every public detail safe to disclose, especially work connected to DMC Connect and Nexora.
5. Make the experience responsive, keyboard accessible, and fast on static hosting.
