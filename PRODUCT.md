# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring teams, technical collaborators, and prospective clients reviewing Teeraphat Raksawong's engineering work. They need to understand his role, ownership, and evidence of delivery quickly.

## Product Purpose

Present Teeraphat as an AI Lead Engineer and end-to-end product builder. The portfolio should make his selected products, applied AI work, research, and engineering approach easy to scan, then provide direct paths to the CV, public project evidence, and contact details.

## Brand Personality

Precise, practical, inventive. Copy is direct and grounded in evidence, with plain English that reflects working communication rather than claiming native or professional fluency.

## Anti-references

- Generic developer portfolio templates and tool-logo walls.
- Unsupported AI claims, invented metrics, or vague case-study outcomes.
- Publishing confidential DMC Connect or Nexora details, customer information, private data, or internal screenshots.
- Dark-gradient, glassmorphism, or decorative motion that obscures content.

## Design Principles

1. Show shipped work and evidence before listing tools.
2. Make Teeraphat's ownership and decision-making clear in every case study.
3. Use motion and geometry to guide attention, not to compete with the work.
4. Keep the site readable, fast, keyboard accessible, and usable with reduced motion.
5. Make every public claim traceable to an approved project, repository, or document.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, semantic structure, visible keyboard focus, minimum 44px touch targets, meaningful image descriptions, and reduced-motion support. The portfolio is English-only.
